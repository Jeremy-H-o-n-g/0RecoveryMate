# RecoverMate Usability Study Consent Script (v1)

**Status:** v1, drafted in S03. Use to obtain informed consent/assent before any session. For participants who are minors, obtain appropriate **parental/guardian consent AND participant assent**. *Obtaining actual consent is student manual work — pending; this script is the instrument.*

## What the study is
A formative usability study comparing two ways to find general recovery/warm-up guidance on a website: an interactive body map vs a simplified list. This is **usability research, not a medical study.** RecoverMate gives general educational guidance only; it does not diagnose or treat.

## What we collect
- Task outcomes (whether you found the guidance), timing, selections, and observer notes.
- Optional general feedback.
- You are recorded only by an anonymous **participant ID**.

## What we do NOT collect
- No names or contact details tied to your data.
- No health, injury, or medical history.
- No diagnosis.

## Your rights
- **Voluntary:** you may decline or stop at any time, for any reason, with no penalty.
- You do **not** need to perform any movement that is uncomfortable — this is about navigating the site, not exercising.
- You will **not** be asked to report pain.

## Data handling
- Data is anonymized, stored offline, and used only to improve the design. Raw data is kept read-only; results are reported as group summaries, never identifying individuals.
