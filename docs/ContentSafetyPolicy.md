# RecoverMate Content Safety Policy

1. General educational recovery/warm-up guidance only.
2. No diagnosis, treatment, injury grading, individualized advice, or guaranteed outcome.
3. Publish only professionally reviewed content with source, license, reviewer, date, captions, transcript, and stop signals.
4. Users should stop and seek qualified assessment for severe pain, swelling, inability to move or bear weight, numbness, or symptoms that persist or affect performance.
5. Do not collect health history or production body-part selections.
6. Research is navigation/usability research, not medical-effectiveness research.
